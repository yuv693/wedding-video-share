# Cancellation & Refund Policy — Wedding Video Share

**Effective date:** 5 November 2026

Wedding Video Share charges **₹290** when a customer chooses to share an uploaded wedding video. Payment is processed by Razorpay.

## Before payment
Uploading a video does not itself trigger the ₹290 sharing charge. If you decide not to share the video, do not select the paid sharing option. You may manage or remove the Wedding Video Share copy in your own Google Drive.

## Failed, duplicate or incorrect payments
If your bank or payment app shows a debit but the app does not show a confirmed payment, do not pay again immediately. First allow time for the payment provider to update the transaction. If the amount remains debited and the order is not confirmed, contact support with the order reference and payment reference. Never send your UPI PIN, card PIN, password or OTP.

If you are charged more than once for the same order, contact support with the relevant payment references so the duplicate transaction can be investigated.

## Refund requests
Refund requests are reviewed based on the transaction status, whether the sharing service was activated, the circumstances of the request and applicable law. A successful payment does not remove any consumer rights or remedies available under applicable law. Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through the payment provider to the original payment method, subject to provider and bank timelines.

## Cancellation after payment
If the sharing link has not yet been issued, contact support promptly and include your order reference. If the link has already been issued or the download has started/completed, explain the circumstances; the request will be reviewed under applicable law and the facts of the case.
