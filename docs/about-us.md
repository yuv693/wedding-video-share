# About Wedding Video Share

Wedding Video Share is a service designed to help customers share a wedding video privately with family and guests.

A customer connects their own Google Drive, uploads one wedding video, and chooses whether to share it. After the ₹290 payment is verified by the payment provider's server notification, the service provides a private guest link and PIN.

Guests use the link and PIN to download the video. The service checks the number of bytes streamed by its server before marking the transfer complete. This check does not prove that the receiver's device saved the file to disk.

The customer keeps control of the video in their own Google Drive. The app never deletes the original video on the customer's phone. Automatic deletion of the service's Drive copy happens only when the customer has separately consented and the server verifies completion.
