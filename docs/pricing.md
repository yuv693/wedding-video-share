# Pricing — Wedding Video Share

## One wedding video sharing cycle

**Price: ₹290 per customer account**

The ₹290 charge applies when a customer chooses to share one uploaded wedding video.

- A customer can select and upload one wedding video before paying.
- Payment is requested when the customer chooses **Share Video – Pay ₹290**.
- A private guest link and six-digit PIN are issued only after Razorpay payment is verified by the server.
- One account covers one wedding-video upload/share cycle.
- To share another wedding video, the customer must create a new account and pay ₹290 again.
- Maximum supported video size: 40 GB (40,000,000,000 bytes), subject to available Google Drive storage and service limits.

The video is stored in the customer's own Google Drive and uses that account's storage quota. The app does not delete the original video from the customer's phone.

For cancellations, failed or duplicate transactions, see the [Cancellation & Refund Policy](/cancellation-refund-policy/).

The displayed price is the service price. Any applicable taxes or charges, if any, will be disclosed before payment as required by law.
